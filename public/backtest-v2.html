Honest caveat — this is not the Phase A4 test the spec asked for

The spec assumed Alpha Vantage's HISTORICAL_OPTIONS endpoint returned real historical chains on the free tier. The A1 audit (docs/options-data-audit.md) showed that endpoint is Premium-only. No free source provides historical option chains.

This backtest substitutes realized_vol_30d for IV. That's a known- weak proxy: realized vol lacks the event-vol pump that pre-earnings IV carries (typically ~1.5× realized in the week before a report). The negative result here is therefore not evidence that real IV won't help — it's evidence that realized vol does not help.

Phase A3 (calibrated stack) and the full Phase A4 backtest are gated on either (a) a paid Polygon Options Starter month or (b) ~6 months of yfinance forward-accumulation per the audit doc.
